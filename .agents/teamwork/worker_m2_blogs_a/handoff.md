## 2. Logic Chain

1. **Problem Analysis**: Google AdSense flags articles under 1,500 words as "thin content," particularly when formulas or calculations lack educational context, realistic scenarios, and local market applications.
2. **Design System Integration**: To expand content authentically without visual degradation, all additions adopted existing design tokens and components defined in `assets/css/styles.css` and `assets/css/blog.css`:
   - `.formula-block` and `.formula-block.simple` for mathematical expressions.
   - `.img-text-block` and `.img-text-block.reverse` with `.img-slot-stat` and `.img-slot-icon` for editorial pacing.
   - `.table-wrap > table.blog-table` with `.highlight`, `.danger`, and `.exact` classes for responsive data presentation.
   - `.callout` and `.callout.warning` for important statutory and risk warnings.
   - `.scenario-card` for interactive budgeting breakdowns.
   - `.faq-accordion` with interactive JavaScript accordion toggles.
3. **Monetization Architecture**: Two in-article ad containers (`.ad-placeholder.ad-slot.ad-in-article`) were inserted at natural reading breaks (~30% and ~70% marks), and the desktop sidebar unit was upgraded to `.ad-placeholder.ad-slot.ad-sidebar-sticky`. Both include standard AdSense `<ins class="adsbygoogle">` tags and valid ARIA accessibility attributes (`aria-label="Advertisement" aria-hidden="true"`).
4. **Navigation & SEO Synchronization**:
   - `blog.js` dynamically binds anchor IDs by matching `<h2>` text content with `<script id="toc-map" type="application/json">`.
   - Every `<h2>` heading in both articles was strictly mirrored in both the `.toc-list` HTML markup and the `#toc-map` JSON array.
   - Complete Schema.org JSON-LD structured data was placed in `<head>`, providing simultaneous `Article` and `FAQPage` rich snippet data.
5. **Verification**: Executed programmatic word count scripts, DOM nesting syntax parsers, and the project's official E2E test suite (`tests/e2e/run_tests.py`), confirming that 100% of the tests touching these files pass.
