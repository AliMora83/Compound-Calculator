# CompoundCalc — Brand Voice Guidelines
**Version 1.0 · July 2026**
**Sources analysed:** Email welcome sequence copy, blog article briefs, calculator UI copy, footer/legal copy, Sprint 11 editorial design direction, project positioning documents.

---

## 1. Brand Essence

**One line:** CompoundCalc makes the maths of long-term investing visible, honest, and South African.

**Positioning:** The free tool a smart friend would send you — not a bank, not a broker, not an influencer. It shows you the numbers and lets the numbers do the persuading.

The Sprint 11 editorial design direction (warm paper, Fraunces serif, ink rules, single deep green) is the visual expression of this same idea: **calm authority over hype**. The voice must match the design — if the site looks like a well-set financial journal, it must read like one.

---

## 2. We Are / We Are Not

| We Are | We Are Not |
|--------|------------|
| **Plainspoken** — "Your balance goes up. Your money buys less." | Jargon-heavy or academic |
| **Quietly confident** — states facts, lets maths persuade | Hype-driven ("10X your wealth!!"), salesy |
| **Honest to the point of sobering** — "It's a bit sobering. But better to know." | Falsely reassuring or motivational-poster fluffy |
| **South African, specifically** — SARB, TFSA, Satrix, rand examples, debit orders | Generic global finance content with ZAR pasted in |
| **On the reader's side** — "remove the decision from your hands entirely" | Preachy, judgmental about past financial choices |
| **Transparent about money** — affiliate disclosures upfront, "not financial advice" plain | Hiding commercial interest in fine print |

**Confidence: High** — these patterns appear consistently across the email sequence, blog briefs, and UI copy.

## 3. Voice Constants vs Tone Flexes

**Constant (never changes):**
- Second person, direct address ("your projection", "your money")
- Concrete rand figures over abstractions (+R28,400, not "significantly more")
- Short sentences for the punchline. Longer ones for the setup.
- Compliance clarity: "not financial advice", "not a registered FSP" — stated plainly, never buried
- The signature move: **a small thought experiment followed by an uncomfortable truth** ("Every month you delay is a month of growth that never happens. It doesn't catch up later. It's just gone.")

**Flexes by context:**

| Context | Formality | Energy | Numbers density | Example |
|---------|-----------|--------|-----------------|---------|
| Calculator UI copy | Low | Neutral | High | "The cost of waiting" |
| Email sequence | Low-medium | Warm | Medium | "Here's a quick thought experiment." |
| Blog articles | Medium | Steady | High | "A 3% difference in return can double your final balance over 20 years." |
| Legal/disclosure | High | Flat | None | "For informational purposes only." |
| Najia_Os social | Low | High | Low-medium | Hook-led, one number per post, always links to the tool |
| Partner outreach (10X, ooba) | Medium-high | Confident | High (traffic data) | Data-led pitch, peer-to-peer tone |

**Confidence: High** for UI/email/blog; **Medium** for social (limited existing samples) — recommend Najia_Os captions get a dedicated pass once more posts exist.

## 4. Language Rules

**Do:**
- "Projection", "scenario", "breakdown" — the tool vocabulary
- ZAR formatting: R1,2 million or R28,400 — always localised
- Name real SA instruments: TFSA, Satrix 40, debit order, SARB inflation rate
- Verbs that give agency: adjust, check, see, compare, start

**Don't:**
- "Guaranteed", "will grow", "returns of X%" as promises — always "could grow", "at an assumed rate"
- "Wealth hacks", "secrets", "tricks" — cheapens the editorial position
- Exclamation marks in product copy (allowed sparingly on social)
- Em-dash overload or emoji in blog body copy (emoji OK in email/UI accents: 📩 ⏳)

## 5. Sample Rewrites

| Off-brand | On-brand |
|-----------|----------|
| "Supercharge your savings with our amazing calculator!" | "See what your money could be worth in 10, 20, or 30 years." |
| "Don't miss out on compound interest!" | "Waiting one year costs more than you think." |
| "Invest now for guaranteed growth" | "At 10% a year, R1,000 a month becomes R765,000 in 20 years. Check the maths yourself." |

## 6. Decisions (resolved July 2026)

1. **Najia_Os voice — DECIDED:** Najia speaks in a light first-person layer over the house values. Same honesty and number-led substance as the site, delivered with more warmth and personality.
   - **First person, active:** "I ran the numbers on waiting one year. Eish." / "I plugged R1,000/month into the calculator — here's the part nobody tells you."
   - **Same signature move as the site:** thought experiment → uncomfortable truth, compressed to hook length.
   - **One number per post.** The number is the star; Najia is the messenger.
   - **Never:** promises, "guaranteed", hype verbs. Najia is honest first, warm second — never salesy.
   - **Every post lands on the tool:** "Check your own numbers → compoundcalc.co.za" (or link in bio).
2. **Vernacular — DECIDED:** No SA colloquialisms in site or blog copy. Sparingly permitted in Najia_Os social captions where natural (fits her first-person warmth), monitored against engagement. The editorial site stays restrained.
